# Prism Warm Palette

A warm luminous recolor of the Prism rail: umber darkness frames amber gates, coral comets, cream cores, and gold lock progression.

## Visual language
Deep burgundy space, restrained HDR highlights, and the original prism geometry and motion, recolored into an amber/coral/cream spectrum. Procedural grid letters carry the same warm language.

## Musical language
96 BPM, compact bell and low-pulse material, shimmer delay, simple scale motion, and basic lock, fire, hit, kill, miss, and reject sounds.

## Mechanical signature
A 30-second run with three enemy kinds, fan-built waves, spiral, zipper, and bloom offsets, and simple per-kind scoring.

## What to study here
Study how a restrained palette change can preserve an authored rail choreography while shifting the mood: dark base, thin HDR accents, and gold lock-count escalation remain legible even with bloom disabled.

# Prism Warm Palette

A familiar prism rail recast as a heated glass corridor: deep umber gives the flight its depth while amber ribs, coral comets, and cream-hot cores turn each fan wave into a small sunrise. The recognizable signature is the warm diamond reticle and the gold flash that climbs with every lock.

## Visual language
Deep umber and burgundy space, restrained amber and coral additive accents, warm cream cores, gold lock progression, glowing prism ribs, star dust, and the original gate, comet, and echo silhouettes. The geometry and movement stay deliberately glasslike; only the palette changes the temperature of the world.

## Musical language
A 96 BPM bell-and-low-pulse score with the original two-chord shimmer, delayed bell trails, downbeat pulses, quantized lock notes, volley bass, and melodic kill lane. The second pass extends the same compact phrase into a 60-second replay arc without changing its musical vocabulary.

## Mechanical signature
A 60-second run built from the original spiral gates, zipper comets, and bloom echoes. Seven authored fan waves repeat as a second cycle: broad opening sweeps, denser middle waves, and a compressed eight-gate finale. Every target uses the same close rail anchors and motion signatures as Prism Bloom.

## What to study here
This is a controlled palette study: the rail, silhouettes, offsets, fan counts, movement patterns, tempo, and synth design are inherited from Prism Bloom, while umber, amber, coral, cream, and gold establish a distinctly warmer identity. It is useful for isolating how much perceived character comes from color and restrained HDR intensity alone.

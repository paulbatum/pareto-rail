# Vespers

Fly the midnight nave of an immense black cathedral and hunt the flat shadows stealing its stained glass. Every kill returns one saturated pane to the architecture, so the building behind the player becomes a permanent ledger of jewel light before the dead rose window erupts in the finale.

## Visual language
Near-black piers, tiered arcades, candle constellations, rib vaults, lead tracery, and an enormous west rose are held almost monochrome against isolated cobalt, blood-red, bottle-green, violet, rose, and gold glass. Ordinary enemies are readable as charcoal silhouettes wrapped around the single colour they stole. Kills shatter that colour outward and relight a matching window for the rest of the run.

## Musical language
96 BPM, 24 bars, and no percussion. A D-minor organ pedal opens alone; cantus, alto, and tenor enter one at a time in imitative counterpoint, with choir and bells reserved for structural swells. Bars 14–18 strip everything back to one flute-stop line. The mixture rank is held back until the rose breaks and the final harmony turns to D major. Locks, shots, armor hits, volleys, and melodic kill lanes are all registered organ voices inside the same harmony.

## Mechanical signature
A 60-second light-restoration run with peeling shades, swinging censers, diving black angels, a deliberately empty tenebrae span, and a west-window boss. Six coloured rose petals must be cleared before the eight-hit, three-stage heart can be locked; breaking it permanently ignites every pane and triggers the level's largest visual, musical, camera, and particle event.

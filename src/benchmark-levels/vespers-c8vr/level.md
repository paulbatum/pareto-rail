# Vespers

You fly the nave of a massive cathedral at night while something is eating the light out of it. Flat black shapes carry stolen stained-glass window panes in their chests. Destroying them restores jewel light to the cathedral's lancet windows, illuminating black stone piers and ribbed vaults in dynamic cobalt, crimson, emerald, and gold. The soundtrack is a live polyphonic pipe organ running counterpoint in D minor, modulating to D Major as the Rose Archon dies and the grand west rose window ignites in a lit cathedral.

## Visual language
Black stone piers, stacked arcade arches, triforium galleries, and ribbed vault ceiling in deep night contrast against saturated stained glass windows. Enemies are flat obsidian silhouettes carrying stolen stained-glass color cores. The reticle is a gothic rose wheel matching the lock radius, illuminating quadrant nodes on lock.

## Musical language
Pure cathedral pipe organ polyphony in real counterpoint with no percussion — the pulse is the counterpoint moving. Organ voices enter layer by layer (Pedal 16', Tenor flue, Alto principal, Choir swells). Player locks, fires, and kills are organ pipe voices retuned to the live D minor organ harmony, with kills executing melodic runs along 32-step organ kill lanes. The finale opens the TUTTI ORGAN rank and modulates to D Major (Picardy Third).

## Mechanical signature
60-second rail flight through a cathedral nave with 3 distinct enemy kinds (Gargoyle Vane, Archon Shard, Seraph Panestealer) plus the Rose Archon boss. Mid-run features a long quiet span with a single solo organ flute voice. The boss fight features multi-stage HP and hostile homing glass shard attacks, culminating in a cathedral-wide stained-glass ignition.

# Tinker Ball

A bright cleanup run across one oversized, cluttered worktable. You are a rolling ball that starts marble-sized among buttons and pins and ends melon-sized, gathering rulers and jars. Dark glue monsters wear stolen supplies as bodies around visible black adhesive cores; cracking a core breaks it into pieces that scatter across the table, stick to your ball, and record every enemy dismantled. Warm lamplight, honey wood, and eccentric pop carry the whole run to a three-core glue-spill finale.

## Visual language
Honey-wood worktable under warm desk-lamp pools, with scratches reading as roads. Button/spool beetles with pencil legs, cardboard-and-clothespin flapping birds, marching ruler stilters, and jiggling glue blobs, all built around glossy black cores. Kills shatter into persistent table debris that visibly sticks to the growing player ball; cream button-cell letters on brass spool rings.

## Musical language
132 BPM, three acts, C-major pop: bell mallets, clipped reed-organ offbeat stabs, bouncy square-wave bass, handclaps, woodblocks and shaker. Transport-quantized player sounds with a hidden two-bar kill-melody lane per act, glue-drum core chips that escalate with damage, and a duck-and-peal finale when the last glue snaps clean.

## Mechanical signature
A 60-second run with scuttling beetles, orbiting birds, marching stilters, wobbling blobs, and a three-core glue-spill boss (4 HP each) whose cracked shells shower the route with collectible debris while the ball dips to scoop through it.

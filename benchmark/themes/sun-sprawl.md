# Sun Sprawl

Build a frantic aerial chase through a dense retro-futurist city at high noon. Pursue an evil megacorporation's armored hauler from crowded lower skyways to executive towers above. The rail dives between skyscrapers, slips under transit bridges, climbs stacked traffic lanes, and banks around rooftop gardens as flying cars stream past. Fill the city with white concrete, painted metal, turquoise glass, civic banners, green terraces, yellow and gold accents, and hard blue sky. Golden sunlight flashes across every turn, giving the spectacle a bright, warm, luminous daylight feel.

Traffic drives the chase across skyways. Corporate traffic-enforcement drones form temporary gates; shoot bright control nodes to open the route. Bounty and security skiffs flank the rail with crossfire and telegraphed ramming runs that press the safe line toward buildings and traffic. Hijacked cargo barges span whole skyways, spilling crates, construction foam, and loose machinery into the hauler's wake. Give every hazard a bold silhouette and readable corporate warning color amid civilian traffic. Keep the hauler as the clear destination throughout.

Score the ascent with a kinetic orchestral-electronic blend: rapid strings, brass stabs, crisp breakbeats, airy synth chords, and an accelerating engine-like bass pulse. Build layers as the route rises and defenses intensify; gates, close passes, and falling escorts punctuate the arrangement.

Boss: the hauler batters through traffic, deploys rear-rack gates, and sends security skiffs to guard flank armor locks. Break the locks on weaving side passes; each section sheds plating and burning debris, driving the hauler into a faster, increasingly erratic climb. The armor-shed hauler races above the tallest towers for a final sprint. Reduce the finale to wind, sparse percussion, and bassline as the drive core opens between engine surges. Land the finishing volley near the corporate spire, sending the hauler across a rooftop landing field to a stop at the sunlit edge while traffic flows safely around it.

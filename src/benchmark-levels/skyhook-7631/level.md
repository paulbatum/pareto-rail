# Skyhook

Ride a storm-battered climber up a space elevator, through the cloud deck and into orbital night. The sky sheds color and sound as the car climbs; then the Cable Reaver appears high on the tether and grows from a distant silhouette into a hull-ending threat.

## Visual language
Storm grey, sunlit blue, indigo, and black belong to the atmosphere. Climber hardware stays utilitarian: white ceramic panels, graphite cable machinery, faded steel, and small hazard-orange service marks. Clouds and debris fall past the car, the planet's curve emerges below, and the orbital station opens around the final approach.

## Musical language
At 96 BPM, a wide noise bed, suspended pads, hull pulses, rain ticks, and cable rhythm lose one layer at every altitude boundary. The boss replaces the missing air with sparse winch percussion and cable strain; docking leaves one sustained chord and two beacons. Locks, volleys, and kills remain transport-quantized notes drawn from the live harmony, so the player supplies the melody as the backing score disappears.

## Mechanical signature
A 60-second, four-integrity climb with broad wind-rider sweeps, orbiting vacuum skimmers, tether crawlers that launch interceptable harpoons, and boarding claws that visibly dive into the climber. The Cable Reaver descends along the tether behind three armored clamps; expose and break its three-stage winch core before it reaches the car, then ride the clear two-bar docking coda into the station.

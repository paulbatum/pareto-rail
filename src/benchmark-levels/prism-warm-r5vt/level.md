# Prism Ember

A warm luminous variant of Prism Bloom: deep umber corridors, amber and coral prism targets, and gold lock progression. Gates, comets, and echoes sweep through fan waves over a glassy rail at 96 BPM.

## Visual language
Warm prism targets — torus gates, trailing comets, orbiting echoes — in amber, coral, gold, and warm cream. Deep umber background with amber-coral ribbed rail lines and warm star field. HDR glow on thin highlights; restrained intensity preserves frame readability.

## Musical language
96 BPM, compact bell and low-pulse material, shimmer delay, simple scale motion, and lock/fire/hit/kill/miss/reject sounds using the same arrangement and harmony as Prism Bloom.

## Mechanical signature
A 30-second run with three enemy kinds (gate, comet, echo), fan-built waves, spiral/zipper/bloom movement patterns, and per-kind scoring.

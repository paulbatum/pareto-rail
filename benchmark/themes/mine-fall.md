# Mine Fall

Build a level where you are trapped in a runaway minecart tearing through a world made from blocks. Start above ground in a bright geometric valley, all sunlit grass terraces, square-cut cliffs, toy-like pines, and distant waterfalls. The track tips over the rim and the whole world drops away: you plunge into timbered shafts as supports snap behind you, jump broken rails, and switch lanes around falling stone. Keep the ride frantic and physical. The cart rattles, banks, leaves the track for breathless gaps, and always seems one impact from coming apart.

Below, the mine opens into immense lava caverns. Hot orange light rises through black basalt columns and mineral seams burn turquoise in the walls; wood, stone, sparks, and molten rock should read as bold solid shapes rather than tiny surface detail. Music begins with a peaceful folk melody, accelerates with the ride, then slows into a quiet finish as the cart reaches safety. Enemies are strange subterranean scavengers riding crude track rigs, dropping obstacles onto your line and attacking from parallel tunnels.

Boss: a colossal stone burrower erupts beneath the rails and races the cart through the cavern, smashing whole sections of track ahead with its armored head. It forces rapid lane changes and jumps while exposed crystal seams along its body give you moments to strike back. When the last seam breaks, it crashes into the cavern wall and brings the mine down with it. The cart blasts out into daylight with the cave-in close behind. Race across a fragile trestle toward solid ground as the camera swings around to reveal the bridge collapsing into the gorge behind you.

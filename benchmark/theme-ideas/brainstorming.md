# Theme brainstorming

1. **Whitewater** — Ride a narrow river through red-rock canyons as an upstream dam failure turns the rapids into a moving wall of water, with raiders attacking from bridges and cliffside winches. Hand drums and low strings build toward a final plunge over the falls into a wide, sunlit basin.

2. **Last Freight** — Defend a steam train crossing a desert at sunset, fighting mounted outlaws along its flanks before climbing through smoke toward the locomotive. A spare guitar theme grows into a driving Western score as the train races a demolition crew to the last bridge.

3. **Blackwater** — Follow a distress beacon into a wreck resting beneath the reach of sunlight, where your searchlights reveal flooded rooms, suspended silt, and scavenger machines still dismantling the ship. The final signal comes from a trapped rescue pod, and the escape retraces the now-collapsing route with its lamp following yours.

4. **Curtain Call** — Fly through a grand theater during its final performance, weaving between moving scenery, swinging chandeliers, and hostile marionettes controlled from the flies. A theatrical waltz accelerates until you reach the puppeteer above the stage and bring the entire set down for the closing bow.

5. **Red Harvest** — Skim golden wheat fields beneath enormous autonomous harvesters that have turned on the farming settlement ahead. Dust, dark machinery, and a low evening sun frame a pounding folk-electronic score as you dismantle the lead machine before it reaches the houses.

6. **Faultline** — Fly a rescue drone through a city during an earthquake, clearing falling debris and damaged security machines while streets split and buildings lean across the route. The journey ends inside a collapsing hospital, where you free an evacuation lift and follow it into daylight.

7. **The Wild Hunt** — Race through a moonlit forest pursued by spectral riders whose antlers appear between the trees before their bodies do. Bowed strings and pounding hoofbeats drive the chase toward an ancient stone boundary the hunters cannot cross.

8. **Sporefall** — Descend through a subterranean forest of immense fungi, where each disturbed cap releases a luminous cloud and predators move beneath translucent membranes. Soft mallets and breathy reeds give way to heavy, muffled percussion as a mass spore release turns the final ascent into a glowing blizzard.

9. **Slipstream** — Enter a cliffside air race that turns hostile when the reigning champion deploys armed wingmen among the competitors. Bright racing liveries, white contrails, and taut breakbeats carry a low-altitude pursuit that ends with a duel through the finish pylons.

10. **Dead Stock** — Cross a shuttered department store after midnight as display mannequins step from their plinths and security shutters divide the route. Polished floors, dim cosmetics counters, and distorted easy-listening music lead toward a towering window display that assembles itself into the final opponent.

11. **Contour** — Fly across a flat topographic map as contour lines rise into mountains, rivers cut into the paper, and hostile survey markers redraw the terrain ahead. Plucked strings accumulate with the landscape, ending in a pullback that reveals the entire flight as one inked route across the map.

12. **High Wire** — Ride a circus aerial rig through a performance sabotaged by mechanical acrobats, passing swinging trapezes, rotating hoops, and towers of balancing chairs. A brisk circus march gives way to a near-silent final crossing above the ring before the orchestra returns with the landing.

13. **King Tide** — Defend a coastal town during an exceptional high tide, flying between half-submerged houses while smugglers exploit the flood to break into its sea vault. The water keeps rising throughout the run, turning familiar streets into narrow channels beneath the rooftops.

14. **Matador** — Face a colossal mechanical bull in a whitewashed arena, circling its charges and breaking the red-hot actuators exposed when it skids past. Dry handclaps, heel strikes, and sharp guitar phrases build toward a final charge that carries the defeated machine through the arena wall.

15. **Counterweight** — Travel through the workings of a monumental clock as a broken escapement sends its gears and pendulums out of rhythm. Each repaired section restores another part of a precise chamber-music arrangement, culminating in the clock striking the hour above the sleeping town.

16. **The Vault** — Follow a jewel thief’s escape route through an extravagant bank, past marble halls, descending security cages, and rotating vault doors. Muted trumpet and a walking bass become a tight electronic chase as the stolen diamond passes between escape drones ahead of you.

17. **Tidepool** — Travel at shrimp scale through a tidal pool, weaving beneath anemones and over shell beds while crabs and hunting fish contest the shrinking water. Clear daylight and delicate percussion give way to deep, rolling bass when the returning tide reconnects the pool to the sea.

18. **Ashfall** — Escort the last evacuation convoy out of a city beneath an erupting volcano, clearing fallen masonry and intercepting burning rocks as daylight disappears into ash. The final stretch crosses a blackened orchard, with the convoy’s red taillights providing the only steady reference through the storm.

19. **Cold Front** — Intercept an armored bomber over a frozen sea, using ice ridges and snow squalls as cover while its escorts descend through the cloud deck. Stark whites, dark aircraft silhouettes, and restrained military percussion culminate in a close pursuit beneath a breaking glacier arch.

20. **Monsoon** — Follow a mountain river uphill through terraced fields and rain-soaked temples as storm spirits descend in sheets of water. Bronze bells and rolling drums build toward the summit, where you split the storm cloud and reveal the flooded valley in sudden sunlight.

21. **Backlot** — Chase a rogue stunt vehicle through a working film studio, crossing a Western street, a rain-soaked detective set, and a miniature spaceport without ever leaving the same continuous shot. Each set brings its own score, while visible scaffolding and stage lights keep the whole spectacle recognizably artificial.

22. **Amber** — Fly through a prehistoric forest as rivers of fresh resin spread down the trunks, trapping leaves, insects, and increasingly large predators in transparent gold. The music gradually slows and softens until a final pullback reveals the entire scene preserved inside a piece of amber.

23. **House of Cards** — Climb through a vast card palace while animated suits dismantle the lower floors and rebuild them across your path. Brushed drums and nimble piano accompany a finale in which one carefully placed volley starts a collapse that travels through the whole structure.

24. **Bone Orchard** — Cross a desert burial ground where the skeletons of extinct giants rise out of the sand piece by piece. Hollow percussion and low horns build as separate bones join into a pursuing creature, forcing a final flight through its unfinished rib cage.

25. **Wavelength** — Ride a radio signal through static, tuning past fragments of lost broadcasts while interference takes shape as sharp, stuttering enemies. The fragments gradually resolve into one complete song, and the final shot clears the noise just as the transmission reaches its receiver.

26. **Event Horizon** — Skim the accretion disk of a black hole while pursuing probes appear in distorted arcs around its shadow. A measured electronic pulse stretches into slower, heavier phrases as the route dives inward, then snaps back into tempo during the escape slingshot.

27. **Warp and Weft** — Fly between the threads of a working loom as snags become attacking knots and enormous shuttles cross the route. Your shots free the tangled threads, allowing a tapestry to form behind you until the finished image fills the final view.

28. **Rookery** — Carry a stolen egg back to a seabird colony, following cliff ledges and sea arches while larger birds dive to intercept you. Wind, wingbeats, and a spare woodwind melody give the run a fragile character, ending with a quiet landing among hundreds of occupied nests.

29. **Red Lantern** — Pursue a runaway festival dragon through a crowded hillside night market, slipping beneath awnings and between strings of lanterns while its paper scales become smaller attackers. Drums and bright reed melodies drive the climb toward a rooftop finale above the celebrations.

30. **Hothouse** — Enter a neglected Victorian conservatory where vines have buckled the ironwork and carnivorous plants unfold from ornamental beds. Harp figures and damp, low percussion accompany a journey from delicate glass galleries into a dense central chamber dominated by one enormous flower.

31. **Snowglobe** — Fly through a miniature winter town inside a snow globe, where each shake sends rooftops, snowdrifts, and clockwork toys tumbling across the route. A music-box tune grows into a full waltz before the storm settles and the town returns to stillness beneath the glass.

32. **Fata Morgana** — Chase an apparent oasis across a white-hot desert as distant towers stretch, split, and dissolve whenever you approach. The enemies share that shimmering uncertainty, but their shadows remain solid guides through a journey that ends at one small, unmistakably real pool.

33. **Negative** — Move through a black-and-white city visible only in photographic flashes, with enemies leaving crisp afterimages that reveal their paths between exposures. Dry jazz percussion accompanies the final development of the image, showing the entire encounter as a single long-exposure photograph.

34. **Ironworks** — Follow molten metal through a working steel mill, skimming casting channels and passing beneath hammer presses while industrial machines tear themselves loose. Each production stage adds its own rhythm until the final rolling line turns the accumulated noise into a heavy, synchronized groove.

35. **Heartline** — Pilot a microscopic repair craft through a failing artificial heart, clearing obstructions while enormous valves open and close around the route. An irregular bass pulse gradually becomes a steady heartbeat as you restore circulation, finishing with a clean surge into the arteries.

36. **Bastion** — Fly along the walls of a besieged coastal fortress, breaking grappling lines and siege engines as defenders retreat toward the final gate. Low brass and field drums build toward a fight against a moving siege tower whose bridge is already lowering onto the battlements.

37. **Pipeline** — Ride inside one enormous breaking wave, passing beneath its translucent roof while foam creatures and spinning debris sweep across the water. Surf guitar and rolling percussion build with the wave’s height, then cut to open air as you emerge just before the barrel collapses.

38. **Gossamer** — Escape through a giant spider’s web at dawn, where dew beads magnify the world beyond and every severed thread changes the shape of the route. Plucked strings turn increasingly tense as the spider approaches, ending with a final cut that releases the whole web into the wind.

39. **Wiretap** — Follow an intercepted signal through a Cold War telephone exchange, moving between switching racks, cable bundles, and spinning tape reels while counter-surveillance machines close in. Relay clicks and restrained spy-jazz phrases lead to a final race to transmit the recording before the line is cut.

40. **House on the Hill** — Enter a deserted house whose rooms rearrange themselves each time you pass through a doorway, while familiar furniture returns in increasingly threatening forms. A small piano melody follows you through the repetitions until the final door opens onto the garden at dawn.

41. **Endgame** — Fly across a chessboard at architectural scale while each opposing piece attacks according to its movement: rooks sweep straight corridors, bishops cut diagonals, and knights leap between towers. Harpsichord and low strings accompany a tightening assault that ends with the exposed king falling across the board.

42. **Kintsugi** — Travel through a landscape of broken porcelain, destroying the dark wedges that hold its fragments apart. Each repaired seam fills with gold, and the final view shows an imperfect but complete landscape beneath a clear, pale sky.

43. **Tomb of Kings** — Descend into a royal tomb whose ceremonial defenses still function, passing rotating stone doors, bronze guardians, and chambers flooded with drifting gold dust. Deep drums and restrained choral tones lead toward a burial chamber that opens upward, turning the escape into a rising flight through the monument.

44. **Grand Piano** — Fly beneath the lid of a concert piano during a performance, threading between vibrating strings while felt hammers rise in enormous, precisely timed ranks. Rogue mechanical parts disrupt the piece until the final volley releases a jammed chord and the whole instrument resonates around you.

45. **Ringfall** — Escape along the inner surface of a failing orbital habitat as entire districts detach and drift across the curved horizon. The familiar scale of parks and apartment blocks gives way to the exposed structure beneath them, with a final leap through the widening break in the ring.

46. **Night Ferry** — Guard a small ferry crossing the river of the dead, where figures beneath the water reach for its passengers and distant lanterns mark false landings. Low reeds and the ferryman’s measured oar strokes carry the journey toward a shore that becomes visible only when the last pursuing spirit lets go.

47. **Bee Line** — Defend a hive during a wasp invasion, flying from sunlit blossom fields into warm amber chambers crowded with comb and moving wings. Fast string figures tighten as you pursue the attackers toward the queen’s chamber, then relax into a broad meadow flight once the entrance is secure.

48. **Airmail** — Escort a battered mail plane through a mountain pass in worsening weather, fending off sky pirates while the pilot follows scattered navigation lamps. An intimate acoustic theme grows with each cleared ridge, ending at a tiny illuminated airstrip beyond the storm.

49. **Paper Boat** — Ride a folded paper boat through rain-swollen gutters, beneath parked cars, and into a storm drain where insects and loose rubbish become substantial threats. Raindrops supply the rhythm, and the cramped passage finally opens into a quiet park pond.

50. **Privateer** — Pursue a treasure sloop through tropical shoals, passing reef markers and ruined coastal forts while its crew cuts loose cargo and fires from the stern. Fiddle, hand drums, and low brass drive the chase into a narrow lagoon where the sloop must turn and fight.

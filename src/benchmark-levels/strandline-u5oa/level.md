# Strandline

An oceanic flight threading the trailing tentacles of a colossal bioluminescent jellyfish, freeing the animal from a parasitic infestation. Most of the run is spent banking around massive shimmering strands in sunlit cyan water, until the rail swings wide to reveal the creature's immense bell filling the view like an emerald moon, before plunging inward to tear the parent parasite from the crown.

## Visual language
Deep oceanic palette shifting from sunlit aquamarine and turquoise near the surface to abyssal indigo in the distance. The jellyfish glows with ethereal emerald green, mint, and warm gold bioluminescence along fifty-two swaying procedural tentacles and a breathing translucent dome bell. Parasites present the only discordant note: sickly bruised purple carapaces, toxic magenta fins, and necrotic violet cores. Procedural 5x7 glyphs for START and REPLAY are styled as glowing marine plankton nodes. The run is fully legible and distinct with bloom slider set to zero.

## Musical language
120 BPM deep aquatic progression in common time across 30 bars (exactly 60.0 seconds). Begins with slow ambient oceanic sub-bass pulses and sparse marimba droplets, gradually building momentum with rolling sub-bass and syncopated four-on-the-floor percussion as the player enters the strand forest. Swells into soaring modal harmonies during the wide bell reveal, shifts into aggressive Phrygian tension for the boss encounter at the crown, and culminates in a breathless pause followed by a serene, shimmering D major choral bloom as the cleansed jellyfish drifts into the deep. Every lock, fire, hit, and kill is quantized to the transport grid and pitched from live harmony, with 32-step melodic kill lanes making chained volleys sing real melodic phrases.

## Mechanical signature
A choreographed 60-second rail run featuring three distinct parasitic enemy species (clamped claspers that unspool and corkscrew, sinuous skimmers dashing in wide undulating waves across the screen, and bulbous spore sacs that launch lockable hostile homing stingers) plus the entrenched Parent Organism boss. The boss fight requires clearing three defensive broods to collapse its protective web lattice before the queen core can be targeted and destroyed. Broad screen-space distribution keeps destruction centerness low and sweep engagement high.

## Vespers is built and verified

**What was built** in `src/benchmark-levels/vespers-xc1a/` (plus gallery images under `public/level-content/vespers-xc1a/`):

- **World.** A 470 m nave of near-black stone: piers with baked shading, arcade and gallery tiers, ribbed vaults, a west wall with portal and rose, and a floor of 4,400 flickering candles. Seventy claimable windows plus the rose live in one merged glass mesh with per-window vertex ranges.
- **The mechanic.** Each shade, moth, censer, and petal claims a dark window ahead of it on spawn, preferring its own colour. A kill flies a light streak back to that window, which ignites and stays lit, tinting the surrounding stone, vault lines, and floor pools. Misses leave the window dark. The whole run starts with only the first bays burning.
- **Enemies.** Lancet-shaped shades that peel off the wall and hover, moths with flapping wings crossing in arcs, censers on chains swinging one bar per period and throwing cinders at the low point, and the rose boss: eight orbiting petals gate a two-stage eye that sinks into the glass when wounded, throws a second ring plus dark shards, and ignites the rose when it dies, with a wave of light running back down the nave.

Pedal alone, tenor at bar 2, alto at 4, soprano at 6, choir and tubular bells at 8, one flute voice for the quiet, a walking pedal and tolling bell under the rose.

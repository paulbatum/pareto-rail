A 45-second run with a 3-point hull, node, drifter, orbiter, and lancer waves, interceptable homing bolts, and a staged Crystal Warden boss.

# Rush

Rush is a night flight down a city street canyon built to test how fast this engine can feel. The speed rig is still the point, but the velocity now reads through asphalt lane dashes, tower faces, streetlights, gantries, traffic, FOV pulls, and the shared depth-reprojection blur.

## Visual language
Near-black low-poly city geometry with flat-shaded road, curbs, sidewalks, and skyscraper walls. Small cyan windows, cyan-white headlights, amber streetlights, taillights, and beat-strobing gantries provide the hot accents without turning bloom into a whiteout.

## Musical language
170 BPM and deliberately blunt: a relentless kick, closed hat, and one distorted bass figure. Player actions answer with terse zaps, ticks, and rejects inside the same harsh electronic palette.

## Mechanical signature
A 30-second speed-feel testbed with a long rail, authored speed surges, simple pods, strafing darts, and heavier armored targets. Scenery cars are non-interactive speed cues: same-direction traffic gets overtaken fast, while oncoming headlights close at absurd speed.

## What to read
- `src/levels/rush/tuning.ts`
- `src/levels/rush/gameplay.ts`
- `src/levels/rush/visuals/index.ts`
- `src/levels/rush/post-fx.ts`
- `src/levels/rush/audio.ts`

## Status & notes
Technical test fixture demonstrating high-speed engine features (e.g., rail pacing); however, as a stripped-down, limited testbed, it is excluded from the level picker and must not be used as a reference for level design quality or distinctiveness.

# Mass Driver

Ride a payload through the bore of a colossal orbital railgun. Every accelerator coil meets the camera on the beat; the widening gaps, climbing electric color, and continuously rising generator hum turn increasing speed into one synchronized sight and sound.

## Visual language
Near-black barrel armor frames 128 thin accelerator coils that climb from arc blue through ultraviolet to charge white. Longitudinal rails make the bore readable with bloom disabled, while skimmers, tri-blade weavers, gimballed sentries, arc bolts, and six massive safety clamps each carry a distinct electrical silhouette. A successful clear splits the muzzle and replaces the tunnel with procedural stars; failure ends inside a white-violet containment burst.

## Musical language
128 BPM for exactly 32 bars and 60 seconds. A locked pulse marks every coil, a three-oscillator bass hum rises continuously across the whole run, and increasingly dense machine percussion follows the speed curve. Player locks, volleys, armor breaks, and kills are transport-quantized and voiced from the live C-sharp-minor harmony; chained kills perform authored melodic lanes. Clearing the interlocks triggers one launch impact and then silence.

## Mechanical signature
A nonlinear speed profile physically spaces each coil farther from the last while preserving one crossing per beat. Three defense-drone families thread, sweep, and orbit between coils; sentries launch interceptable homing arc bolts. At bar 23, six safety interlocks cycle through four increasingly hot breaker banks, each authored as a full sweep-and-release phrase. Clear every bank before the final peak to open the muzzle and launch, or the surviving circuit detonates the barrel and the player.

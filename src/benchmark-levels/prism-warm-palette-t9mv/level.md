# Prism Warm Palette

A warm luminous recolor of the Prism Bloom rail: a deep umber glass corridor catches amber ribs, coral comets, and cream-hot prism cores as the player sweeps through fan waves. Its identity is the contrast between burgundy darkness and restrained golden bloom, carried by the same bell-and-low-pulse score.

## Visual language
Deep umber and burgundy form the dark base. Amber and coral separate the three target silhouettes, warm cream keeps the cores and glyphs legible at zero bloom, and gold marks lock-count progression. Thin additive rings and ribs retain restrained HDR intensity so the warm highlights bloom without washing out the frame.

## Musical language
96 BPM, the original compact bell and low-pulse arrangement, shimmer delay, simple scale motion, and the same transport-quantized lock, fire, hit, kill, miss, and reject responses.

## Mechanical signature
The original 30-second Prism Bloom run is preserved exactly: three enemy kinds, fan-built waves, spiral, zipper, and bloom offsets, with the same rail, spawn timing, scoring, and movement.

# Spillway

Build a level where you chase a salvage walker down a mountain river. The walker is a huge four-legged machine that wades down the middle of the river, and it is heading for the old dam at the bottom of the gorge to tear its gates out for scrap. You ride low over the water behind it. Keep the walker in view ahead of you for the whole run: it is the goal, and it should get closer and larger as the level goes on.

The setting is a granite gorge on a clear morning. Grey rock walls, dark pines along the ledges, cold green water, white foam where it breaks over boulders, and sunlight that reaches the river only in places. The gorge starts wide and calm, then narrows into rapids, so the walls get closer and the rail drops, twists, and speeds up with the water. Speed should come from rock, spray, and trees passing close, not from streaks and glow. Keep the machinery industrial: yellow paint worn to bare steel, hydraulic rams, cables, and work lights. Enemies need to stay readable against bright water and pale rock.

The walker defends itself on the move. It launches skiff drones off its back that skim the river and come at you. It also drops winch pods onto the cliffs that fire down into the gorge, and it drags cables across the river behind it that you have to fly through or cut. Early waves are light while the river is open. They get denser as the gorge narrows and you close in.

Boss: you catch the walker at the dam crest, where it has its claws in the gates. Break it down leg by leg and then go after the core, while it swings at you and keeps pulling at the dam. When it falls, the gates give way. Turn the camera to show the reservoir coming through, then let the flood carry you over the spillway and down into a wide, sunlit valley. Everything slows and goes quiet over open water, and the level ends.

Score it with hand drums and low strings, with no synth leads. The calm upper river gets a sparse, plucked opening. The drums and string ostinatos build as the rapids get rougher, and the dam fight is the loudest point. The music drops out at the break, and the full theme returns as you come over the spillway.

# Prism Warm Palette

A luminous recoloring of Prism Bloom that trades cool indigos and cyans for warm earthy tones. Deep umber backgrounds anchor the scene while amber and coral accents dance through the prism structure, with warm cream highlights that bloom softly at the edges. The rail geometry and gameplay remain unchanged, preserving the original's spiral, zipper, and bloom enemy choreography while presenting a new visual identity through restrained warm HDR intensity.

## Visual language
Deep umber (0.25, 0.12, 0.06) forms the dark base and scene background; amber (1.0, 0.68, 0.12) and coral (1.0, 0.45, 0.35) alternate as the main structural accents in the prism ribs and environment; warm cream (1.0, 0.95, 0.85) is reserved for the brightest cores and letter glyphs so thin highlights bloom without washing out the frame. Gold (1.0, 0.82, 0.15) anchors the lock-count progression on the reticle and enemy types.

## Musical language
The audio structure and all instrumentation carry over unchanged from Prism Bloom: the same harmonic framework, beat quantization, kill lanes, and synth character remain authoritative. Palette is visual only; the soundtrack delivers the musical identity.

## Mechanical signature
Unchanged from Prism Bloom: three enemy kinds (gate, comet, echo) moving through spiral, zipper, and bloom patterns; rail-anchored motion; volley-based scoring. The gameplay arc and timing decisions are identical — only the visual presentation shifts to warm luminous tones.

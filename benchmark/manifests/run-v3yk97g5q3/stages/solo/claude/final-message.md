**Thermal Ink is built and passes every headless gate.** The level lives entirely in `src/benchmark-levels/thermal-ink-yk97/` — a 60.0-second continuous boss fight (24 bars at 96 BPM, ending on the phrase boundary) against a mutant octopus wrapped around a capsized hull in a sodium-lamp harbor.

**How the level works**

- **The ink/infrared rhythm is one clock.** `timing.ts` defines three bar-aligned ink windows (bars 6–8, 12–14, 20–23) plus `inkAt`/`infraredAt` ramp functions. Gameplay staging, the ejected cloud blobs, the fog/material mode-swap, the TSL screen grade (charcoal grayscale with preserved red signal cores and scanlines), and the music's section turnovers all read the same windows — each blackout has a ~0.9s blind beat before the lens snaps in.
- **Boss structure.** The body is scenery anchored 30 units ahead of the camera (always on screen while the rail circles the basin), with eight writhing dressing arms, slit lamp-amber eyes, and a siphon. The six lockable arm tips (2 HP each, red signal cores) unfurl in pairs across the run; the core (`hitStages [2,6]`) spawns just before the final ejection and is only lockable once **all arms are dead and the final blackout has begun** — so the killing volley always lands through ink, and the lamps return on the kill.
- **Supporting cast**: skimmers (debris-rays crossing the full screen), lurkers (barnacle crabs with telegraphed spit), dredgers (3-HP jaws lowered in on chains from above the mantle), and interceptable homing ink globs. 3-point hull.
- **Audio**: Em–Cmaj7–Am–Bm over an 8-bar cycle; half-time industrial kick, bouncing filter bass, anvil/chain percussion, and one haunting lead that turns bright and focused under infrared while drums fall back to a sub pulse with sonar pings. Kills walk per-mode melody lanes, locks climb the live chord, core chips ring an escalating anvil, the reject is a sour foghorn blat, and the core kill lands a scheduled finale (duck, sub drop, E-minor bloom, falling peal, foghorn).

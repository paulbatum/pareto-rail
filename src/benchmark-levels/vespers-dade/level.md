# Vespers

Fly the nave of a massive black cathedral at night while something eats the light out of it. Every kill wins a stained-glass window back; the Eater nests in the dead rose window holding every colour it has taken — break it open and the run ends in a lit cathedral.

## Visual language
Black stone piers, arcade tiers, ribbed vaults, a candle floor far below; the only saturated things are cobalt, blood-red, bottle-green and gold glass. Enemies are flat black shapes peeled off the glass — moths, gargoyles, swinging thuribles — each with a stolen pane burning in its chest. Gold-leaf START/REPLAY glyphs, gold reticle and bolts, jewel-tinted nave light that warms as windows relight.

## Musical language
The building's own organ in D minor at 84 BPM, no percussion: a lone pedal D, then flute, principal and choir entering voice by voice in counterpoint over 21 bars. Locks, shots and kills are pipe stops inside the polyphony, pitched from the live harmony; chained kills ring a bell rank held back all night. The dark span drops to one voice before the finale opens every rank on D major for the rose ignition.

## Mechanical signature
Three enemy motions (drifting moths, lunging gargoyles that spit homing cinders, pendulum thuribles) plus orbiting rose petals guarding a 6-HP Eater core. 3-point hull, volley-multiplied scoring, full lock/unlock/fire/hit/kill/miss/reject choreography, window-relight persistence across the run.

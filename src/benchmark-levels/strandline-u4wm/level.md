# Strandline

A sixty-second flight through the tentacles of a gigantic jellyfish. Violet parasites cling to its green-gold strands; clearing the infestation restores the animal's light. A wide bank reveals the bell before the rail returns to the strands and climbs toward the infected crown.

## Visual language
Clear teal water, deep-blue distance fog, shafts of sunlight, translucent oral ribbons, beaded tentacles, and a ribbed green bell. Parasites use violet flesh and pale pink cores. Gold lock rings, geometric glyphs, expanding water ripples, and green release particles remain visible without bloom.

## Musical language
96 BPM, twenty-four bars. A slow sine pulse and suspended D-minor chords gain plucked overtones and filtered water percussion. Locks follow the current chord; kills perform written melodic lanes above the backing register. The crown strips the score down, and destroying the parent resolves into D-major ninth harmony with a descending density of sound.

## Mechanical signature
Clasp parasites detach from short strand sections, ribbon parasites swim laterally, and armored urchins orbit while rotating. Nine brood targets feed three web sectors around the parent. Each brood kill removes part of its sector; clearing all nine exposes a two-stage, six-hit parent. A six-target clear earns a bonus. Killing the parent triggers a continuous camera retreat until the complete animal fits in view. An uncleared colony produces a distinct failed-rescue summary without shortening the sixty-second run.

# Selfsame

This one is not a place. Do not build a world; build a rule, and let the rule make everything the player sees.

The rule is that everything here is one shape, and every one of those shapes is built from smaller copies of itself. Which shape is yours to choose, and the choice is most of the assignment — it has to stay legible when it is the enemy, the architecture, and the horizon all at once. You fly inward. You pass through an opening in the structure and come out inside another one exactly like it, larger, and then again, and again, and the level never says whether you are shrinking or the world is growing. No ground, no sky, no horizon, and nothing in frame of a size the player already knows — nothing to measure themselves against. That is the level: no scale, no place, no story, and total coherence anyway.

The enemies are the same shape at whatever size they happen to be — what you killed in the first seconds is what the structure around you is made of. The structure is still; anything that moves is alive, and a hostile must read as hostile from its motion alone, because nothing else will tell you. Distinguish the kinds by how they move and how they come apart, never by what they are.

Hold the palette to almost nothing — one material, one accent, deep contrast, no neon — then move it by rule: every time the world nests, the whole frame takes one even step — in hue, in temperature, in light, whichever, but always the same step. By the end the player is somewhere they could not have predicted from the start, and they got there in steps they never felt.

The music is made the way the world is. One short figure — short enough to hum — repeated on several voices at slightly different rates, sliding out of alignment with itself and coming home again: process music, mallets and organ tone, no drums doing the ordinary job. Keep one voice locked to the pulse the whole way, so the player's own notes always have a floor to land on. Each time the world nests, another voice enters at another rate. The figure is never once abandoned; everything that happens is that figure heard from further away.

Ending: you reach the middle, and the middle is not a middle. The view pulls back, and the entire structure the player just flew through is one small shape inside the next one, and it is still going.

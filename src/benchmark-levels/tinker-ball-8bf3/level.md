# Tinker Ball

Tinker Ball is a bright, tactile clean-up run across one oversized wooden worktable. A marble-sized ball rolls through warm desk-lamp pools, dismantling dark glue creatures assembled from buttons, rulers, spools, and clothespins; every core break showers the route with rescued supplies that cling to the growing ball. Its recognizable signature is the collision of cozy workshop clutter with a hot pink-and-gold pop score and a final purple glue spill.

## Visual language
Deep walnut, paper ochre, coral cloth, turquoise thread, and small HDR lamp filaments keep the table warm while nearly-black adhesive cores give every enemy a readable center. Scratches act as roads through the tabletop. Beetles are button shells on twitching legs, stilts are ruler-legged walkers, birds are snapping clothespins with flapping wings, and spools wobble around exposed cores. The player ball starts as a faceted marble and grows toward melon scale, collecting debris on its uneven surface. Three spreading glue puddles, each wrapped around a fresh supply layer, resolve into a spotless final patch.

## Musical language
128 BPM bright eccentric pop: a round sine kick, clipped reed-organ chords, a bouncy saw/triangle bass, handclap-like glue pops, tiny high-passed workshop shakers, and bell-like mallets. Four eight-bar sections move from sparse marble taps to a denser desk-drawer shuffle, a forward lamp sprint, and a suspended glue-spill finale. Lock notes and lethal hits are quantized into the live chord's lead set, so a careful volley plays a melodic run rather than a stack of identical effects.

## Mechanical signature
The 60-second rail has four choreographed waves of increasingly large supply-bodied enemies, spread across the full screen rather than held in one lane. The final three cores are a sequential boss: each takes two locks in two stages, and the next core is sealed until the previous one is cracked. Kills spawn short-lived scatter fields plus two pieces that arc onto the ball, making the player's route visibly record the cleanup.
